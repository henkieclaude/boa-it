FAQ

Questions about ripping online

What is an online card rip?

You buy a sealed booster pack from a website, and the site opens it for you on camera. You keep whatever is inside, the same as opening it yourself, without waiting for the pack to ship first.

Do I actually get the cards?

Yes. Every site we list lets you ship the physical cards to your address. Most also let you keep them in a free vault until you are ready, or sell them back for credit.

Are Japanese packs better to rip than English ones?

Japanese packs are usually cheaper per pack and release sets earlier. English packs hold more value for many collectors in the West. Several sites on our list stock both, so you can choose per set.

How do you make money?

We earn a commission when you sign up or buy through our links. This never changes the price you pay. Sites cannot pay to change their ranking.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. Most rip sites require you to be 18 or older, especially those with buyback or credit features. Check the rules in your country before signing up.
